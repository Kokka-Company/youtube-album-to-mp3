YouTube to MP3

Paste a playlist or video link. Each track is saved as a 320 kbps MP3 with artist, album, track number and cover art written into the file.

More options

YouTube demands a signed-in session for some albums. Auto borrows your browser's YouTube login only if that happens.

Already-downloaded tracks are skipped, so you can safely re-run the same link.
